## Chosen Direction: Field Notes & Fine Lines

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial utilitarianism**, informed by horticultural field journals, property survey markings, and modern independent-service brands.

### Core Principles
1. Make expertise tangible through structured labels, service notes, and practical visual detail.
2. Contrast generous calm space with crisp, information-rich components.
3. Keep every customer action obvious, without making the dashboard feel corporate or technical.
4. Use natural texture and asymmetry to make the experience feel rooted in a real place.

### Color Philosophy
The palette begins with **North Eastern Green**—a nearly black evergreen that conveys trust, competence, and year-round care. Creamy paper tones keep the interface open and residential; chartreuse is reserved for action, schedule status, and progress, mirroring fresh-cut grass without becoming decorative. Weathered clay is used sparingly for earthbound warmth and prioritization.

### Layout Paradigm
The landing page follows a **field-sheet composition**: a narrow information rail, large editorial content zones, and intentionally offset modules rather than a centered card grid. The portal is a **living job board**, with the next service acting as the visual anchor and supporting account information organized in a modular right-hand ledger.

### Signature Elements
1. Fine topographic linework and coordinate-style labels.
2. A cut-grass stripe motif used as a visual divider and progress language.
3. Small stamped service markers that make dates, estimates, and status feel physical.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel useful and precise: buttons depress subtly, route transitions are calm, and information reveals in short, contextual layers. A request for service never drops a customer into an empty form; it begins with a guided property context.

### Animation
Use 160–240ms snappy ease-out transitions for hover, navigation, and controls. Reveal page sections with 30–60ms staggered opacity/translate entrances; show schedule progress with stripe motion only when active. Honor reduced-motion preferences by removing nonessential entrance and decorative motion.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for high-impact, confident editorial headlines and **Manrope** for compact, capable interface text. Headlines are large but not centered by default; labels are uppercase, tracked, and small. Numeric information uses tabular figures, with semibold weights reserved for actions and service dates.

### Brand Essence
**North Eastern Lawn delivers dependable, expertly organized property care for homeowners who want their outdoor space handled with the same standard they bring to the rest of their life.**  
Personality: **grounded, attentive, exacting**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear, local, and practical; CTAs describe the next useful action rather than demand conversion. Microcopy is calm and specific.

> “Your next clean cut is already on the calendar.”

> “Tell us about the property. We’ll build the right routine.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The supplied circular lawn-and-mower emblem remains the primary brand lockup. A compact, no-text mower-stripe mark will act as the digital favicon and dashboard mark, echoing the emblem’s circular composition without competing with it.

### Signature Brand Color
**North Eastern Green — #143C2A**

## Visual Remix — Customer Acquisition Focus

### Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Lawn Lens  
**Very Brief Intro:** Cinematic property photography becomes the navigation system: each section is a visual window with sparse, useful overlays and the actions live in the image itself.  
**Probability:** 0.102

### Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Cut & Collect  
**Very Brief Intro:** Big editorial crops, bold orange accents, tactile receipts, and visual status cards turn a normally quiet service category into something more energetic and memorable.  
**Probability:** 0.168

### Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Before the Weekend  
**Very Brief Intro:** A lifestyle-first concept that sells the feeling of a clear Saturday rather than the mechanics of mowing, using a photo collage and a single, high-confidence quote journey.  
**Probability:** 0.131

### Chosen Direction: Cut & Collect

The redesign moves from an editorial information page to an **image-led acquisition experience**. It uses dramatic photograph crops, a compact color-blocked quote prompt, oversized service imagery, and barely-there supporting copy. Orange is elevated from a minor accent to the action color; evergreen remains the grounding canvas.

The first-time customer portal state will behave like a focused conversion moment rather than an empty dashboard. It will show a visual property-care brief, three illustrated quote steps, and one clear call to action: **Build my quote**. The existing service request form remains the data destination, but the first-use experience will guide customers into it instead of presenting a blank calendar.
